Summer 2026 · pre-collegiate

Stanford Summer of Neurosciences 2026

Stanford Medicine | Neurosurgery · virtual program

Virtual neuroscience program (July 7 – July 30, 2026) built around three parts: a weekly Journal Club analyzing primary research papers, Career Insights sessions with clinicians and researchers from across the field, and select Stanford Neurology and Neurosurgery Grand Rounds. Summer 2025 · pre-collegiate

Stanford CNI-X 2025 Youngest, rising freshman · Session 2

Stanford Medicine · Department of Psychiatry & Behavioral Sciences · Clinical Neuroscience Immersion Experience (CNI-X)

Selective, application-based two-week immersion (July 21 – August 1, 2025) with Stanford med-school faculty: clinical neuroscience, neuropsychiatry, design thinking via the Saggar lab method, and large-language-models in behavioral health. I was the youngest student in the cohort and the only rising freshman. Our six-person team capstone, A.R.A.I.A., an aphasia-recovery app concept, was presented to Stanford faculty, staff, and families. I want to keep developing it carefully, with clinical review before any patient-facing use.

Reading from the program

Brain Facts: A Primer on the Brain and Nervous System (Society for Neuroscience). I’d had this open since 8th grade, and it became the baseline I kept coming back to during CNI-X. Each chapter is short and sourced; a lot of what later showed up in my HarvardX coursework I had already first met here. Read it free on brainfacts.org →
