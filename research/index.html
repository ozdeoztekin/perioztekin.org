Source discipline

How I handle claims

I try to keep three things separate: what a source directly shows, what I infer from it, and what I still do not know. That matters most when a claim is clinical, technical, or ethical.

Source shows

The study, deck, program page, or official source actually states it.

My inference

A careful connection I am making, labeled as my interpretation rather than as a fact from the source.

Still open

A question that needs better evidence, clinical review, replication, or real-world testing.

For clinical projects like A.R.A.I.A., this means the public wording should stay close to the source materials until a licensed clinician, therapist, or formal study supports stronger claims.
