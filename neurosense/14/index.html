Your Brain Cheats Physics to Be Fast

Saltatory Conduction

Action potentials don't crawl down an axon. They leap from gap to gap. The gaps — Nodes of Ranvier — are where the brain's reaction speed lives.

The Science

  • Nodes of Ranvier are the bare gaps between myelin segments. They hold 'a high density of voltage-gated sodium and potassium channels.'
  • The action potential is regenerated at each node, then travels passively through the insulated stretch. The leap is electrical.
  • Without saltatory conduction, sending 1 cm would need an impossible ~330,000 mV.
  • A giant axon would also work, but 'a human arm would require nerve fibers of over a meter diameter.' Myelination is nature's efficient version.

The Protocol

  • Omega-3 fatty acids (walnuts, flaxseeds, fatty fish) — myelin is mostly lipid.
  • Same focused practice as V13 — myelination and saltatory speed are linked.
  • Avoid chronic inflammation (excess sugar, alcohol) — it degrades myelin.
