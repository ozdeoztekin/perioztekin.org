What If Autism Isn’t a Deficit?

The Intense World Theory

The Intense World Theory: autism isn’t a deficit of feeling. It’s a brain whose gain knob is stuck on MAX.

The Science

  • Markram & Markram (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, 2010): hyper-connected, hyper-reactive neural circuits. Same stimulus, much louder response.
  • Lights, ambient sounds, micro-expressions — all of it arrives at full volume. The shutdown in a loud classroom isn’t disengagement; it’s system overload.
  • Reframes autism from 'lack of empathy' to 'too much signal to process.'

The Protocol

  • Name the three loudest 'noises' when overwhelmed (auditory, visual, emotional).
  • Five-minute de-gaining: noise-cancelling headphones or dim lighting. Let the salience network recalibrate.
  • If you’re neurotypical: a shutdown is a SOLAR FLARE of feeling, not the absence of it. Give space, not judgment.
