Pick the Wrong Music and You Sabotage Your Studying

Music & Neural Entrainment

The music you play isn't background noise. It's running your brain.

The Science

  • Thaut et al. (Frontiers in Psychology, 2015): brain wave activity synchronizes to the frequency of rhythmic external stimuli, a phenomenon called neural entrainment. Music is the most powerful everyday entrainment input.
  • Will & Berg (Neuroscience Letters, 2007): EEG recordings show cortical oscillations measurably phase-lock to periodic acoustic rhythms. Faster tempos drive higher-frequency cortical activity associated with focus; slower tempos drive lower-frequency patterns associated with rest.
  • Tierney & Kraus (Brain and Language, 2013): rhythmic ability correlates with cognitive and linguistic processing, supporting the idea that rhythm directly engages high-level cognitive networks.
  • Implication: lyrics activate Broca's and Wernicke's areas (your language network), the SAME network you need for reading, writing, and complex problem-solving. They compete with the task.
