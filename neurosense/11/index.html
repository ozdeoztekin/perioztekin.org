Your Brain Is a Biological Battery

The Resting Potential

Move just 1 in 100,000 ions across the membrane and you generate −80 mV. The resting potential is built on the tiniest possible imbalance — and that's the energy behind every thought.

The Science

  • Membrane potential = the voltage difference between inside and outside of the neuron. Resting reads ~−70 mV.
  • It comes from a tiny charge imbalance at the membrane — driven by leakage of K+ down its concentration gradient while large anions stay trapped inside.
  • Neurons range from −40 to −90 mV at rest. The membrane behaves like a capacitor: small ion flows produce big voltage swings.

The Protocol

  • Potassium-rich foods (bananas, leafy greens, beans, potatoes) — K+ is the dominant ion at rest.
  • Magnesium (pumpkin seeds, spinach, almonds) supports the ATPase that maintains the gradient.
  • Hydration matters — every ion concentration is per liter of water.
