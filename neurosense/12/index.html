The Protein That Eats 1/3 of Your Food

The Sodium-Potassium Pump

One pump in your neurons runs nonstop, moving 3 Na+ out and 2 K+ in for every ATP it burns — and it eats ~70% of your brain's entire energy budget.

The Science

  • Cox (Harvard MCB80.1x): 'The sodium-potassium pump continually moves three sodium ions out of the cell, for every two potassium ions it pumps into the cell.'
  • Without it, even a slow trickle of Na+ leaking in would unravel the resting potential.
  • 'Consumes up to 70% of the ATP required for overall brain function… something like a third of everything you eat in a day goes to powering this pump.'

The Protocol

  • Steady glucose: complex carbs over sugar spikes. The pump runs continuously, not in bursts.
  • Prioritize deep sleep — ATP replenishment peaks during slow-wave sleep.
  • Maintain stable hydration: ion concentrations are what the pump is paying to defend.
