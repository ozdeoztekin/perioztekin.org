The After Meal Walk

post-meal movement and glucose

That heavy crash after eating can make your brain feel like it briefly lost the signal.

The Science

  • After a meal, blood glucose rises as digestion sends energy into circulation.
  • Light movement gives your muscles a reason to take up some of that fuel.
  • That can smooth the body signal your attention has to work inside of.

The Protocol

  • Walk lightly for a few minutes after a meal when it is safe to do so.
  • Keep the pace easy enough that it feels like cleanup, not exercise.
  • Check your energy about one hour later instead of judging the first sleepy wave.
